# macOS pilot evaluation

**Status:** Evaluation complete, pilot pending approval\
**My role:** Owner

## Goal

Introduce Macs for a small group, capped at about 10 users: executives, office staff, and people who depend on the ERP and the OEE platform.

## Findings

- **Line-of-business apps.** Business Central and the OEE platform both run in a browser, so neither blocks a move to macOS.
- **MDM.** The company already owns Scalefusion licenses. macOS management needs a higher Scalefusion plan, and upgrading the licenses already owned avoids buying a second MDM. Intune was ruled out on cost. Mosyle was the other option considered.

## Pilot plan

1. Three-device pilot, pitched to IT leadership for approval.
2. Wider rollout only after the pilot is approved.
